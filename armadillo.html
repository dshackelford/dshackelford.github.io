Armadillo Camper · Truck bed canopy

Custom camper canopy built like my drone hardware.

Armadillo is a modular canopy for my truck bed. I treated it like any other mechanical project—model the 8020 frame, fabricate composite panels, iterate hinges, and make it easy to assemble in the driveway.

8020 Extrusions Composite Panels Truck Camper

Armadillo deployed at a campsite.

Design Overview

Mechanical

Frame

  • 8020 aluminum skeleton sized for the Tacoma bed.
  • Precision bracketry so panels can be removed without losing alignment.
  • Modeled joints for both driving and deployed configurations.
Mechanical

Panels & Hinges

  • Composite panels that keep weight down but survive the desert.
  • Custom hinges allow the canopy to fold into “armadillo” mode.
  • Gas struts hold everything open while camping or working.
Mechanical

DIY Friendly

  • Cutdown lists, hardware kits, and step-by-step notes for anyone who wants to copy it.
  • Design makes room for bike mounts, power, and storage in the bed.
  • All fasteners are easy to service from the outside of the canopy.
