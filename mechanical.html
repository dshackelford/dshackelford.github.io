Selected Work

A sample of mechanical projects from Drones Made Easy, Mutable Campers, and earlier hands-on experience.

DJI Camera & Sensor Mounts

Drones Made Easy – Design Engineer (2017–2020)
  • Designed mounting brackets for custom cameras on DJI airframes.
  • Generated detailed manufacturing drawings for machining in aluminum, carbon-fiber, and plastics.
  • Used iterative 3D-printed prototypes to verify clearances, center of gravity, and cable routing.

Mapping Rig Bundles

Drones Made Easy
  • Assembled calibration plates and mounting rigs for turn-key mapping systems.
  • Verified mechanical alignment in real flight scenarios and refined designs based on results.
  • Coordinated with Sony’s agricultural team for multispectral payload integration.

Camper Canopy Structure

Mutable Campers – Freelance Design Engineer
  • Designed a custom “camper canopy” using 8020 aluminum extrusion and sheet-metal panels.
  • Modeled assemblies and joints in FreeCAD with attention to manufacturability and serviceability.
  • Ran simple tolerance checks and geometry analysis using Octave.

Radar Station Hardware Integration

NOAA Radar Station – UCSB Capstone Systems Lead (2016–2017)
  • Selected towers, batteries, and enclosures for a coastal radar station installation.
  • Focused on repeatable components for future deployments on the Channel Islands.
  • Coordinated mechanical, electrical, and software requirements across a five-person team.
